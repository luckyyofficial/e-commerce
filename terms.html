2. Privacy Policy & Cookie Compliance (GDPR & CCPA)

Your privacy is paramount to our hardware engineers and business operations. We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers.

Information We Collect
  • Contact Details: Your name, billing/shipping address, telephone number, and email.

We stand behind the engineering excellence of every device in our store. If you are not thoroughly satisfied with your product, you have 30 days from delivery to request a full refund or exchange.

Return Requirements: Items must include all original packaging, manuals, accessories, power adapters, and protective coverings. Return shipping labels are pre-paid for all VIP Tier members.

All cross-border duties, VAT, and brokerage fees are calculated upfront at checkout (DDP - Delivered Duty Paid). You will never be charged surprise tariffs at doorstep delivery.
